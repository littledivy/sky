# Draft VSX submission — TIC 24695715

**Submission type:** New variable from archival survey data

**Requested submitter/discoverer credit:** Divy

**Primary identifier:** TIC 24695715

**Other identifiers:** Gaia DR3 4697634881027710464; UCAC4 111-001466

**ICRS position (Gaia DR3, epoch 2016):** RA 25.06210645°, Dec −67.81509844°

**Proposed type:** EA: (uncertain; transit-like/eclipse signal). The light curve shows one U-shaped dip per cycle and no clear secondary eclipse. The physical companion type is unknown, so this classification should be reviewed by a VSX moderator.

**Passband:** TESS

**Magnitude and amplitude:** TIC TESS magnitude 12.25; measured dip depth ≈1.31%, corresponding to ≈0.014 mag. Approximate TESS-band range 12.25–12.26; the catalog magnitude is not an absolute calibration of the normalized light curve.

**Period:** 0.240691392 days (5.7766 hours)

**Epoch:** BJD(TDB) 2460883.382786 (BTJD 3883.382786)

**Evidence:** Repeats in TESS sectors 95 and 96. The signal is also recovered in full-frame cutouts from sectors 1, 2, 29, 68, and 69 at the same period. A linear ephemeris across those archival checks and sectors 95/96 has ≈2.4-minute RMS timing residuals. Difference-image localization places the signal within 4.2″ and 2.0″ of the TIC position in sectors 95 and 96.

**Survey references:** TESS-SPOC light-curve products, sectors 95 and 96; additional MAST TESSCut full-frame cutouts from sectors 1, 2, 29, 68, and 69. Supporting plot: [phase-and-timeseries.png](phase-and-timeseries.png).

**Remarks:** No match was found in the five checked published TESS eclipsing-binary tables, VSX within 36″, the NASA Exoplanet Archive TOI table, or exact TIC/Gaia identifier web searches. SIMBAD returns UCAC4 111-001466 at the position. The sector 95/96 SPOC TCE table check remains open because those CSV downloads timed out. No planetary or stellar companion classification is claimed.
